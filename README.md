# 🛒 Supermarket Sales Data Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes supermarket sales data to evaluate business performance, identify sales trends, and uncover actionable insights across products, branches, customers, payment methods, and time periods.

The project combines **data preparation, exploratory analysis, KPI development, data visualization, and business analysis** into an interactive Power BI dashboard.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The main objective of this analysis is to understand:

* How sales are performing over time
* Which branches and product lines generate the most sales
* How customer types contribute to revenue
* Which payment methods are most commonly used
* When sales activity is highest
* What patterns and trends can be identified from the data

## 🔄 Data Analysis Process

The project followed an end-to-end data analysis workflow:

### 1. Data Preparation

Preparing the dataset for analysis and ensuring the required fields were structured correctly.

### 2. Data Cleaning

Handling data quality issues and preparing the dataset for analysis and visualization.

### 3. Exploratory Data Analysis

Exploring sales performance across different business dimensions to identify patterns and trends.

### 4. KPI Development

Creating key performance indicators to measure overall business performance.

### 5. Data Visualization

Designing interactive visuals and dashboards to present the findings clearly.

### 6. Business Insights

Interpreting the results and identifying meaningful patterns that can support business understanding and decision-making.

## 🔎 Key Insights

### 📉 Sales Trend

Sales across the analyzed period showed a declining trend:

| Month    | Sales |
| -------- | ----: |
| January  |  116K |
| February |  109K |
| March    |   97K |

This trend highlights the need to investigate the factors associated with the decline in sales over time.

---

### 🏢 Sales by Branch

The analysis compares sales performance across the three branches:

* Giza
* Alex
* Cairo

Sales were relatively close across the branches, allowing for comparison of location-level performance.

---

### 🛍️ Sales by Product Line

**Food & Beverages** was the highest-performing product line, generating approximately **56K** in sales.

The analysis also compares:

* Sports & Travel
* Electronic Accessories
* Fashion Accessories
* Home & Lifestyle
* Health & Beauty

---

### 👥 Customer Type

**Member customers** generated approximately **190K** in sales, compared with **133.27K** from Normal customers.

This indicates a significant contribution from membership customers to overall sales.

---

### ⏰ Sales by Time of Day

The **Afternoon (12 PM–6 PM)** period generated the highest sales at approximately **172.47K**.

Sales were also analyzed across:

* Morning (6 AM–12 PM)
* Afternoon (12 PM–6 PM)
* Evening (6 PM–12 AM)

---

### 💳 Payment Method

The dashboard compares sales transactions across:

* E-wallet
* Cash
* Credit Card

This provides insight into customer payment preferences.
